# Stillarc Studio privacy policy draft

Draft for version 0.28, 12 November 2026. Publish at a public URL and add a support contact before distribution.

Studio processes imported photos, video, edits and selection analysis on your device. The app contains no advertising, tracking, analytics or account service and does not upload your media to a Studio server. Apple Photos, Files, Share and any destinations you choose operate under their respective providers’ policies.

Studio stores imported project copies and settings locally. Removing a project moves its Studio copy to Recently Deleted for up to 30 days; permanent deletion and uninstalling remove local data. Originals in Photos or Files are not deleted by these actions. Export important media separately; local metadata recovery is not cloud backup.

Exported location, capture date and camera details are optional. Location is opt-in. Technical encoding information remains, and video sound has a separate choice. Review export settings before sharing.

The bundled privacy manifest declares no tracking or collected data, and required-reason access for app preferences, owned/user-selected file timestamps and elapsed timing. These support saved settings, local file management and performance measurement. This draft must be reviewed against the final binary and App Store privacy answers before publication.
