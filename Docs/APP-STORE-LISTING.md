**Name:** Stillarc Studio

**Subtitle:** Photo & video color tools

**Promotional text:** Shape light and color, save your look, and keep every edit reversible. A focused photo and single-clip video studio for iPhone and iPad.

**Description:** Develop photographs and grade video with precise light, color, RGB curves, wheels and channel mixing. Import creative LUTs, convert supported Log footage and save reusable looks. Add natural grain or a warm highlight-edge glow. Refine selected areas with brush, gradient, color and brightness masks, plus on-device subject and sky selections. Crop, straighten and manually correct lens distortion without changing your original.

Trim a clip and export HEVC SDR or 10-bit HLG HDR with audio and metadata choices. Export full-size JPEG/PNG photographs, copy adjustments between projects, save alternate versions and organize with favorites and ratings. Studio stores projects locally; keep exported backups of important work.
